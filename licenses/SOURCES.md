# Upstream notices

`objc2.md` is the unmodified upstream licensing notice from
https://github.com/madsmtm/objc2/blob/8852b424193ca41602281b3d7540d7c8ed51e49a/LICENSE.md.
The registry packages omit this repository-level file. The same notice is present
at the revisions recorded by the locked block2, dispatch2, objc2, objc2-encode,
Foundation, CoreFoundation, and Metal packages.

`valuable.md` is the unmodified upstream license from
https://github.com/tokio-rs/valuable/blob/9efc29b6e58cef28f6566a47aa7e142a55fead77/LICENSE,
the revision of the locked valuable 0.1.1 package, which omits it.

Bundles also include license files from the resolved registry packages,
their Cargo license/repository metadata, and the host/compiler Rust copyright
notices from the installed toolchains.
